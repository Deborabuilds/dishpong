DishPong

A small simulated culture of spiking neurons plays Pong. A miss floods it with random noise; a hit gives it a calm, predictable beat. It learns to make the beat happen.

How it works: the ball's height is delivered as spikes on 16 sensory electrodes. 16 motor neurons each vote for a paddle position. Synapses leave eligibility traces; a hit (calm, predictable input) consolidates the traces that were active, a miss (noise) weakens them and shakes the synapses up in proportion to how often it's failing. Calm → exploit, noise → explore.
